Project: CampusDesk - university IT support routing pilot
Synthetic demonstration materials. All people, dates, volumes and budgets below are fictional.

Objective: Reduce the time required to route student and staff support tickets to the right IT team.
The pilot covers password-reset requests, classroom equipment faults, network access issues, and software installation requests. It runs for one campus before any wider rollout.

Inputs: A CSV dataset of historical tickets containing customer data, email addresses, issue descriptions, and previous team assignments.
The dataset was assembled from exports supplied by several departments. Departments use different category names, and their ticket histories cover different academic terms.
Output: An automated routing decision assigns each incoming ticket to a support queue immediately.
Workflow: Read incoming ticket -> call external model API -> write team assignment -> send a notification to the assigned queue.
The model is provided by a third-party vendor. The application will use cloud hosting and paid inference for each ticket.
Target: 4,000 users at launch, with traffic expected to increase during registration week.
The team intends to deploy to production after a short demonstration with department coordinators. Pilot results are not attached to these materials.
